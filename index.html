Inbox Zero - Divergent Design Studio

A private, self-hosted installation of the open-source Inbox Zero email application, operated by Sean Fischer / Divergent Design Studio for personal use.

The application uses Google sign-in and, with the account owner's authorization, Gmail access to display, organize, search, and manage email. AI-assisted email features can use a separately configured model provider.

This is an informational site only. It does not host the email application, expose a mailbox, accept sign-ups, or provide access to private email data.

This installation is independently operated and is not the hosted Inbox Zero service. Google account access is granted through Google's consent screen and can be revoked in your Google Account settings.
