Privacy policy

Inbox Zero - Divergent Design Studio
Effective November 2, 2026

Operator and scope

Sean Fischer / Divergent Design Studio operates this private, self-hosted Inbox Zero installation for the operator's own authorized accounts. It is not a public email service. This policy describes that installation, not the separately operated hosted Inbox Zero service.

Google account data

With the account owner's consent, the application accesses basic Google profile information, email address, Gmail messages and metadata, labels, and relevant Gmail settings. Requested Gmail permissions allow reading, composing, sending, organizing, and modifying email and settings. Actual operations depend on the features the owner enables. The installation currently keeps outgoing email disabled; permission to send does not mean messages are automatically sent.

Purpose and processing

Data is used to authenticate the owner and provide requested email features, such as displaying and searching messages, organizing labels, managing email settings, and optional AI-assisted classification or drafting. Application records, authorization tokens, and feature-related email data may be stored in the self-hosted application's database, caches, and operational logs. The application uses cookies or equivalent session storage for sign-in.

AI providers and other services

If the owner configures an AI provider and enables AI features, relevant email content, metadata, prompts, and instructions may be transmitted to that provider to perform the requested task. An AI provider has not yet been configured for this installation. Provider-specific privacy, retention, and processing terms apply when a provider is selected. Google receives the requests necessary for Google authentication and Gmail operations. Other integrations require separate configuration or authorization.

Limited use and sharing

The operator's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used by the operator to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Transfers to a configured processing service are limited to providing the owner's requested application features. Data may also be disclosed where legally required.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Application data is held in the operator-controlled deployment. It is retained as needed to provide the enabled features and until the owner removes it or deletes the deployment's stored data; feature-specific cleanup may remove some records sooner. Operational logs and backups, if made, can have separate lifetimes. This policy does not promise immediate deletion from backups or independently operated providers.

The owner can disconnect an account within the application where supported, revoke access through Google Account connections, and remove locally stored application records and volumes. Revoking Google access prevents future authorized access but does not itself delete previously stored local data. For access, correction, or deletion requests, contact the operator below.

Security

The deployment uses restricted local configuration files and application encryption secrets for sensitive records. Its web interface is currently bound to the operator's local machine. No method of storage or transmission is guaranteed completely secure. Credentials and private email data are not intentionally published on this informational website or its public source repository.

This public website

This static informational website is hosted by GitHub Pages and contains no application sign-in, mailbox contents, advertising scripts, or analytics scripts added by the operator. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, under its privacy statement. Visiting this website does not connect a Google account.

Changes and contact

The operator may update this policy as enabled features or providers change. The effective date above identifies the current version. Contact: divergentdesignstudio@gmail.com.
